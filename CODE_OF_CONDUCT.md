# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be kind and specific.
- Assume good intent while still naming real problems.
- Avoid sharing private family data, child images, or voice samples in public project spaces.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, threats, doxxing, sexualized language, and discriminatory conduct are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or block participants who do not follow this code.
